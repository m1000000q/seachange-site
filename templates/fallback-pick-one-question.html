Pick one question: how to scope a data pilot

The fastest way to prove what joined-up data can do is not a big BI project. It's one question your team can't answer quickly today.

Magui Millon, Co-founder[Date] · [X] min read

Most small teams already have the data they need. It just lives in too many places: a CRM, a finance system, marketing tools and a handful of spreadsheets. None of them agree, so a simple question takes days to answer, and the team ends up reporting on the business instead of growing it.

The temptation is to fix everything at once. A new tool, a big dashboard project, a data warehouse. That's usually where momentum dies. We do the opposite.

Start with the question, not the tool

A good pilot question is one your team asks often, can't answer quickly, and would act on if it could. For example:

  • Where's my pipeline stuck?
  • What's driving growth this quarter?
  • Which customers are most valuable, and how did we win them?

If the answer would change what someone does on Monday morning, it's a good question.

If it has data and a question behind it, we can help you join it up.

The pilot in three steps

  1. 1Pick one question. The one your team can't answer quickly today.
  2. 2Connect the data. We join up the systems behind it and clean what's needed, starting with agreeing what "a lead" and "a deal" mean in your business.
  3. 3Review the answer. Together we decide what to act on and what to build next.

What you get at the end

An answer, the number behind it and what to do next. Plus a clear view of which other questions the same data can answer. Because the pilot starts by understanding how your business really works, every number has a meaning, and the next question is quicker than the first.

That's the sea change: insight made simple.
