About

Introducing SeaChange

SeaChange was founded by two marketers who kept seeing the same gap: marketing running campaigns while sales felt unsupported and unheard. We believe in the commercial trifecta, with sales, marketing and leadership working to the same goals. At the heart of it is the data that shows what works.

Advances in AI now make it possible to bring that data together quickly, without manual reports or long BI projects. We join up your systems into clear dashboards you can talk to, so you can see what to do next.
